Bundle and promotion ideas

  • Consider a bundle of a frequently purchased item with a slow-moving item, after checking that both have positive margins.
  • For products nearing expiry, use a clearly labelled, optional discount that does not sell expired or unsafe goods.
  • Compare supplier quotations before reducing prices; the lowest unit price may not be the lowest delivered cost once MOQ and delivery charges are included.
  • Do not apply a blanket discount across the entire inventory. Review each product's cost, margin, stock and expiry date.
